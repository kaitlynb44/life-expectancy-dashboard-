# life-expectancy-dashboard-
Interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing health and economic factors affecting life expectancy across 10 Asian from 200-2015.  

## Objectives 
- Analyze life expectancy trends across Asian Countries
- Examine the relationship between income and life expectancy
- Explore the impact of health risk factors including HIV prevalence and alcohol consumption
- Compare GDP and adult mortality rate across Asian countries
- Communicate findings through an interactive Tableau dashboard

- Tableau
